Learning languages the right way

Dr. Avellaneda

Dr. Avellaneda is a pedagogue, linguist, designer, and researcher with over 30 years of experience. He currently teaches at the University of the District of Columbia, where he serves as expert faculty in the teaching of bi/multilingual students whose first language is not English. An avid traveler, he speaks Spanish, English, French, and Italian, and is currently learning German.

Welcome

Hi, and welcome to The Language Clinic. I'm Dr. Avellaneda.

You're about to enter a new kind of language-learning experience — one where the target language you learn is authentic, taken directly from movies, TV shows, and real interviews with native speakers.

Every activity here is designed to help you practice and internalize a carefully, intentionally selected set of target language. Listening comes first; linguistic awareness supports the rest of the learning experience. Vocabulary and grammar support the process — but they aren't the focus. Real use is.

Please select the language you're interested in learning.

— Dr. Avellaneda
