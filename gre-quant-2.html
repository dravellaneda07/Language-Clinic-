GRE · Quantitative Reasoning · Lesson 2

Number Sense

Number sense is the toolkit behind almost every GRE Quant question: signs, fractions, percents, ratios, powers, and the order of operations. Learn the ten key ideas from the video, then show what you learned with self-graded tests of ten questions, one from each topic.

Part 1

Learning

Instructions: Watch the video several times, take notes when appropriate, then do the exercises in Part 2 to demonstrate your learning.

Note-taking guide

The video covers ten topics, and every test in Part 2 has one question from each topic, in the same order. For each topic, write the rule in your own words and one example. You can write in English or your first language.

  1. Integers
  2. Absolute Value
  3. Operations with Fractions
  4. Decimals and Percent
  5. Ratios, Rates, and Proportions
  6. Powers
  7. Scientific Notation
  8. Rounding
  9. Factors and Multiples
  10. Order of Operations

Part 2

Practice Tests: Demonstrate Your Learning

Instructions: Each test has 10 questions, one from each topic in the video. Choose one answer (A–D), then click Check answer. Work on paper first. Each question is worth 1 point, and you get one attempt per question, so check your work before you click. Read every explanation, even when you are right. When you finish, click See my results, then Next test for 10 new questions. There are 10 different tests (100 questions), and no question repeats until you have seen all of them.
